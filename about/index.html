About us

Good lighting.
Good people behind it.

Led by Light is a lighting and grip company serving the Pittsburgh area. We bring together a comprehensive equipment package and experienced crew to help you get the look you want, with practical support from setup to wrap.

01

Cost effective.

Production costs add up quickly. We help you get the equipment you need at an affordable cost, so more of your budget can go toward what ends up on screen. A great look starts with the right tools and the right plan.

02

Convenient.

Less time digging through gear. More time getting the shot. Our truck is equipped with carts and a liftgate for efficient loading and unloading, with an organized layout that keeps equipment accessible throughout the day.

03

Complete.

Modern LED lighting meets proven industry staples. Lights, stands, frames, fabrics, and flags come together in one package, giving you options to shape the light and adapt as the needs of your production change.
