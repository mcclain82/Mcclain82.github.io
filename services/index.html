Equipment Rentals

Build a package around your shoot. Our inventory brings together modern LED fixtures, established lighting staples, and the grip equipment needed to shape and support them. Whether you have a detailed gear list or need help choosing a setup, we can work through the options with you.

  • Fisher dolly package and optional accessories
  • Equipment selection based on your location, look, and budget

Lighting and Grip Crew

Experienced people make the equipment work for your production. Our lighting and grip crew works with your cinematographer and production team to build the look, manage the setup, and make adjustments as the day moves from shot to shot.

  • Gaffer services and hands-on lighting support
  • Grip support for shaping, cutting, and bouncing light
  • Equipment preparation, setup, and wrap
  • Practical problem-solving as locations and shot requirements change

Rigging & Power

Get the lights into position and plan how to power them. We help work through mounting, support, cable routes, and distribution around the needs of your location. From stage setups to vehicle work, the approach starts with the shot and the practical demands of the space.

  • Stage and vehicle rigging
  • Lighting mounts, stands, and support hardware
  • Power distribution setup and cable management
  • Planning for access, available power, and equipment placement

04

Control & Data

Bring your fixtures together with a control system that suits the shoot. We handle DMX setup and programming so lighting changes can be made from the board, whether you need a simple adjustment, a repeatable cue, or coordinated changes across multiple fixtures.

  • Wired DMX and wireless CRMX setup
  • Fixture addressing, patching, and data distribution
  • Lighting cues, looks, and programming
  • Board operation and on-set control support
